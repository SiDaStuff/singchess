# Using Sing Chess

Sing Chess is a browser chess-training application at https://chess.singdevelopments.com/.

## Review a completed game

On Home, select Paste PGN, enter the completed game's PGN, and choose Review game. The Lichess and Chess.com import tabs accept public usernames and let you choose a recent completed game. Review uses the same board and controls as the rest of the application. Select a move in the move list to inspect it. Browser engine review downloads Stockfish when needed.

## Puzzles and Coach

Open Puzzles from navigation to practice tactics. A free account saves training progress. Coach requires sign-in and has a plan-dependent allowance shown in the interface. Engine analysis and Coach output can contain errors; move classifications and estimated game ratings are not official player ratings.

## Play through Lichess

Open Play, sign in to Sing Chess, and choose Connect Lichess. Authorize only through Lichess's own consent page. Choose an available time control and casual or rated play. During a game, submit moves on the board and use the displayed game controls. After the provider confirms the game has ended, Review Game opens the completed PGN in the review workspace.

Never use analysis or Coach assistance during an active online game. Do not submit moves, start matches, change subscriptions, or modify account settings without the account holder's authorization.

## Account controls

Settings contains board, sound, analysis, and notification preferences. Account shows saved progress and plan details. Public username imports are distinct from the authenticated Lichess Play connection. Automatic reviews require the eligible plan and an explicit opt-in for the connected Play account.

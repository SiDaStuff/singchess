Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what Sing Chess collects and why. We keep it simple: we only store what we need to run the service, and we don't sell your data.

What we store

  • Account data. Your email and a Firebase user ID if you sign up. Passwords are handled by Firebase; we never see them.
  • Games and reviews. PGNs you paste or import, reviews you save, and automatic reviews created for eligible completed Lichess Play games.
  • Training and coach data. Your puzzle rating, solve history, synced coach conversations, coaching preferences, response feedback, and coach-game results. Coach messages and relevant chess context are sent to the configured AI provider to produce a reply.
  • Fair-play data. Fair-play reports contain game-derived screening signals. Reports with stronger indicators may enter an administrator queue for human review, including reviewer notes and whether the reviewer agreed, disagreed, or found the result inconclusive.
  • Connected chess accounts. Public usernames you save for imports. If you connect Lichess Play, we store your Lichess identity and an encrypted OAuth credential so the server can make authorized Board API requests. The credential is never sent to your browser.
  • Preferences. Board theme, piece set, engine settings, and similar options. These live in your browser by default and sync to your account when you're signed in.
  • Technical data. IP address and browser type for rate limits and security. We also keep anonymous counts like total games reviewed.

Where your data lives

Firebase Authentication handles sign-in. Application account data is stored in our server database, with backups in Firebase Realtime Database. Firebase may process data on Google infrastructure, including in the United States. Local preferences stay in your browser unless you sign in and sync them.

Cookies and local storage

Firebase uses browser storage to keep you signed in. A device-identifier cookie and hashed IP records help prevent abuse and multiple accounts. The site also uses Google Analytics to measure page visits and usage; your browser may store analytics identifiers. Hosting and security providers may process IP addresses and request logs. You can clear local site data in Settings or your browser; this does not delete data held on the server.

Third parties

  • Firebase — sign-in and database backups. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Lichess — public imports and authorized Play account, game, move, and challenge requests. See the Lichess privacy policy.
  • Chess.com — public games and ratings imported by username. See the Chess.com privacy policy.
  • AI provider — Coach messages and relevant chess context are processed to generate a reply.
  • Google Analytics — page and usage measurement.
  • Google reCAPTCHA — bot detection on the signup and contact forms. Google processes the request per their privacy policy and terms.

We don't sell or share your personal data for advertising.

Public profiles

If you choose a username, a public profile appears at chess.singdevelopments.com/profile/<username>. It shows your username, plan flair, puzzle rating, and puzzle stats. Your email, games, and usage stats stay private. You can hide your profile by contacting us.

Messages

When you use the contact form, we keep your email and message so we can reply. If you're signed in, we also store your user ID. Only site admins can see these.

Retention and your choices

Notifications are removed after 30 days. Automatic reviews retain the 20 most recent reviews. Fair-play reports follow the retention period shown on the report; review labels may be retained longer in de-identified calibration statistics. Other account data is kept while your account is active or as needed to provide the service, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You can disconnect Lichess Play, unlink public usernames, clear local browser data in Settings, or ask us to access, correct, export, or delete your account data through the contact form. Some requests may require identity verification.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the contact form or email the address on the home page.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

This policy explains what Sing Chess collects and why. We keep it simple: we only store what we need to run the service, and we don't sell your data.

What we store

  • Account data. Your email and a Firebase user ID if you sign up. Passwords are handled by Firebase; we never see them.
  • Games and reviews. PGNs you paste or import, plus any reviews you save to your account.
  • Training data. Your puzzle rating, solve history, coach chat history (if saved), and coach-game results.
  • Preferences. Board theme, piece set, engine settings, and similar options. These live in your browser by default and sync to your account when you're signed in.
  • Technical data. IP address and browser type for rate limits and security. We also keep anonymous counts like total games reviewed.

Where your data lives

Account data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database). Local preferences stay in your browser unless you sign in.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. Firebase may set an authentication cookie or local storage entry to keep you signed in. To help detect multi-accounting and abuse, we set a single device-identifier cookie and store a hash of your IP address; raw IP addresses are not kept. You can clear all stored data from Settings → Clear data or through your browser.

Third parties

  • Firebase — sign-in and database.
  • lichess.org and chess.com — only when you import your public games by username.
  • Google reCAPTCHA — bot detection on the signup and contact forms. Google processes the request per their privacy policy and terms.

We don't sell or share your personal data for advertising.

Public profiles

If you choose a username, a public profile appears at chess.sidastuff.com/profile/<username>. It shows your username, plan flair, puzzle rating, and puzzle stats. Your email, games, and usage stats stay private. You can hide your profile by contacting us.

Messages

When you use the contact form, we keep your email and message so we can reply. If you're signed in, we also store your user ID. Only site admins can see these.

Your choices

You can use most of the site without an account. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the contact form or email the address on the home page.
